# Morning briefing

Every morning, a short briefing shows up in the Claude app on your phone. It covers what's due, what changed, what's happening in your classes today, and what to work on. You can read it in under a minute.

## What it's for

You have deadlines in Canvas, changes in your email, events in GroupMe, classes that get canceled, and things to do outside of school. Checking all of that yourself every morning takes too long, and something always slips. The briefing pulls it into one place, puts the most important thing first, and tells you plainly where you stand.

## How to use it

Once it's scheduled, you don't have to do anything. Open the Claude app in the morning and read it.

You can also ask for it any time:

- "What's due today?"
- "Give me my briefing."
- "What do I have this week?"

Here's what it covers, top to bottom. Empty sections are left out.

- **The one thing that matters most today.** If something changes your classes today, that comes first.
- **Class today:** a class that's canceled or moved, something to bring (a calculator, your lab kit), or something to do before class.
- **Due today**, with anything overdue marked plainly.
- **Readiness:** exams coming up that your practice says you aren't ready for, and missing work from Canvas.
- **Today's schedule:** your classes, meetings, and events in order, plus a heads-up about conflicts (two things at once, a party the night before an exam).
- **Due this week** and what's coming up.
- **Tasks:** what's open on your to-do list, and which ones fit into today's free time.
- **Changes** since yesterday, including new grades and instructor comments.
- **Exams:** a countdown for each one.
- **Focus:** one or two sentences on what to work on today, for about how long, and why.
- Problems with Oso's connections, if there are any, in plain words.

On Sundays, the briefing also looks back at last week, sums up how heavy the coming week is, and includes a note on how you've been practicing.

## Getting the most out of it

- **Read it before your first class.** That's where "bring your lab kit" and "class is online today" pay off.
- **Act on the Focus line.** It weighs your grades, how close each exam is, and how ready you are, so you don't have to.
- **Answer its questions.** If it asks which course a clipping belongs to, or when a class meets, reply in the same chat and Claude takes care of it.
- **Shape it to fit you.** Say "from now on, give me the briefing as bullet highlights" or "start every briefing with the weather," and Claude offers to keep that as a rule. See [Rules](rules.md).
- **Take its trade-offs seriously.** If it suggests skipping a party to study for tomorrow's exam, it says why: your grade, your practice scores. It only suggests; you decide.

## Setting it up

The briefing is a scheduled task in Cowork that runs every morning at the time you choose. See [Install guide: Schedule the morning briefing](../install.md#schedule-the-morning-briefing).

## Good to know

- It's honest, not encouraging. It leads with what's behind and doesn't praise you unless your numbers show it.
- It never makes up a deadline, a date, or a grade weight. Everything comes from what Oso has actually seen.
- It runs in Claude's cloud, so it arrives even when your laptop is closed. It reads what your laptop last sent to Google Drive, so if the laptop has been off for a day, the briefing says it's out of date.
- If it doesn't show up, see [Troubleshooting](../troubleshooting.md).
