# Class schedule

Oso knows when each of your classes meets and keeps them on your calendar. When an instructor cancels or moves a class, the calendar changes too.

## What it's for

Canvas knows your assignments but not when your classes meet. Without class times, Oso can't tell you a class was canceled, can't find study time that avoids your classes, and can't remind you of something when class lets out. With them, your calendar shows your real day, kept up to date.

## How to use it

When you set up a course, Claude reads the class times from the syllabus: every lecture, lab, and discussion, with the days, times, and room. It also gets the first and last day of classes and any days off. If the syllabus doesn't list them, Claude asks you. Your registration schedule has them.

For a course you set up before Oso knew about class times, the morning briefing asks for them. Just reply:

- "Physics is Tuesday and Thursday 9:30 to 10:45 in Baylor Sciences 101, lab Wednesday 2 to 4:50 in room 210. Classes end December 4."
- "No class the week of Thanksgiving."

Your classes go on the Oso calendar, three weeks at a time, and Oso keeps adding the next weeks as they come.

When a class changes, you don't have to do anything. Oso reads your school email, GroupMe, and Canvas announcements and messages. If it sees "no class today," "we're meeting online Thursday," or "lab is in room 140 this week," it changes just that one class on your calendar. It also mentions the change at the top of your briefing.

To change your times for the rest of the term, tell Claude the new ones ("my Wednesday lab moved to 3 PM"). Future classes move, and anything already canceled stays canceled.

## Getting the most out of it

- **Give every meeting.** Labs and discussion sections matter as much as lectures, both for your calendar and for finding open time.
- **Include days off.** Holidays and breaks keep phantom classes off your calendar.
- **Use it for reminders.** "Remind me to swing by the mail room on my way back to the dorm" pops up on your phone when the class you're heading to lets out. See [Tasks](tasks.md).
- **Let it protect your study time.** Study blocks from your [rules](rules.md) and study plans are placed around your classes.

## Setting it up

Class times are part of setting up each course. See [Install guide: Set up your courses](../install.md#set-up-your-courses). Your classes show up on your calendar once the Oso calendar is connected. See [Install guide: The Oso calendar](../install.md#the-oso-calendar).

## Good to know

- Class events don't come with reminders, so your phone doesn't buzz before every class.
- If a cancellation arrives some way Oso doesn't read, like said out loud in class or posted on a website, tell Claude: "Physics is canceled Friday."
