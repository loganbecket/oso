# Setting up

The **Set up Oso** window takes you through everything after the installer, one step at a time, so you don't have to work through the install guide on your own.

## What it's for

Getting Oso running means a handful of things in different places: telling Oso where your vault is, connecting Canvas, adding the Oso plugin to Claude, setting up your courses. The setup window lists every step down the left side and shows one at a time, with what to do and a button for anything Oso can do itself.

## How to use it

The installer opens it when it finishes installing. The first steps are needed before Oso can do anything:

1. **Your notes folder**: choose your vault (the one you made in Obsidian, inside your Google Drive folder). Your time zone is filled in from your computer; change it if it's wrong.
2. **Canvas calendar**: paste your Canvas calendar feed address. The window says where to find it.
3. **Sign in to Canvas**: press **Sign in to Canvas** and sign in as usual. The step ticks itself off once you're in. (On Linux, Canvas sign-in isn't available; skip it.)

Once your calendar feed is in, Oso starts checking for changes on its own, even if you stop there.

The rest are things you do in Claude or elsewhere. Each says what to do; press **Done** when you've done it, or **Skip for now**:

- **Add Oso to Claude**: install the Oso plugin in the Claude app.
- **Google in Claude**: connect Google Drive, Calendar, and Gmail in Claude's settings.
- **The School project**: the Cowork project where every study chat starts.
- **Your first course**: set up a course from its syllabus with `/create-course`.
- **Calendar, email, and tasks**: Oso's own Google calendar, your school email, and Google Tasks, with a button to connect each.
- **GroupMe**, **Morning briefing**, **Claude in Chrome**, and **reMarkable tablet**: optional extras.

**Back** returns to an earlier step. Closing the window loses nothing: every step is remembered the moment you finish or skip it.

## Coming back to it

Until every step is done or skipped, the Oso window asks each time it opens whether you want to pick up where you left off. Choose **Resume setup** to carry on, or **Not now**. Tick **Don't show this again** to end setup for good; anything you never did stays on the Oso window's Status tab, with a button beside it.

Once every step is done or skipped, setup is over and the question never comes back. From then on, connections are managed from the Status and Actions tabs of the Oso window (see [Looking after Oso](looking-after-oso.md)).

## Getting the most out of it

- **Do the first three steps in one go.** With the vault and Canvas in, Oso has your deadlines on its first check.
- **Skip what you don't use.** GroupMe, Chrome, and the reMarkable are only for students who use them. Skipping costs nothing, and the Status tab still offers them later.
- **Set up one course, not all of them.** The first course is enough to see Oso working; add the rest whenever you have their syllabi.
- **Keep the installer's window open** until you close the setup window; it finishes the install after that.
