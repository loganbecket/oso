# Deadlines and alerts

Oso keeps one list of every assignment, quiz, exam, and reading across all your classes, with due dates and grade weights. When something important changes, it tells you right away on your calendar.

## What it's for

Deadlines come from too many places: the syllabus, Canvas, an email from your instructor, an announcement in class. They also move. Oso combines them into one list, removes duplicates, and watches it for changes, so a moved exam doesn't catch you off guard.

## How to use it

Ask Claude:

- "What's due this week?"
- "When is the physics midterm?"
- "Mark the chem lab report as done."
- "I started the essay."
- "The quiz on Friday is worth 5%."
- "Move HW 6 to Monday, the professor extended it."

Your changes stick. The next time Oso checks Canvas, it keeps your edits.

**Where deadlines come from:**

- your syllabus, when you set up a course
- the Canvas calendar feed, which every Canvas account has
- Canvas itself, if you've signed in to Canvas through Oso (this adds grades and weights; see [Canvas](canvas.md))
- your school email and GroupMe, if they're connected (see [School messages](school-messages.md))

The same assignment from two sources shows up only once.

**Urgent changes** reach you before the next morning briefing. That includes a moved due date, a rescheduled exam, or a new graded item due within the next week. Within about 15 minutes, Oso puts the change on the Oso calendar in your Google Calendar, with reminders a day before and two hours before. Everything else waits for the morning briefing.

## Getting the most out of it

- **Keep your statuses current.** Telling Claude "done with HW 4" keeps the briefing focused on what's actually left.
- **Give weights when you know them.** Oso uses them to decide what matters most, and to answer "What do I need on the final to get a B?"
- **Set quiet hours.** In the Oso window's Settings tab, set quiet hours (for example 10 PM to 7 AM). Alerts that come in overnight wait until morning.
- **Mute a course you don't need alerts for.** In the Settings tab, a muted course stays on your list but doesn't send alerts.
- **Mark finished courses.** Say "mark Calculus II finished" at the end of a term. It drops out of the briefing and alerts but stays searchable.
- **Watch for crunch weeks.** The dashboard flags a week with a lot due, or two exams, so you can start early.

## Setting it up

The Canvas calendar feed is part of installation. Alerts on your calendar need the Oso calendar connected. See [Install guide: The Oso calendar](../install.md#the-oso-calendar).

## Good to know

- Oso only reads from Canvas and your school. It never submits, posts, or changes anything there.
- Oso only writes to the calendar it made, called Oso. Your other calendars are out of its reach.
- What counts as urgent is a setting: a new or moved item due within 7 days, by default.
- Without the Oso calendar connected, urgent changes still show up in your briefing.
