# Tasks

Tasks is your to-do list for life outside coursework: laundry, an oil change, registering to vote, paying intramural dues. It lives in Oso and in a Google Tasks list named Oso on your phone, and the two stay in sync.

## What it's for

These things take time and don't go away just because nobody grades them. They also don't belong on your deadlines list. Tasks gives them one place where you can see them, Claude can work with them, and the morning briefing can fit them into your free time.

## How to use it

Add a task by telling Claude, by typing or by voice:

- "Add laundry."
- "I need to get an oil change this week."
- "Register to vote by Friday."

A task can have a day it should be done by, or no day at all. "This week" means by Sunday.

See and change your list:

- "What's on my list?"
- "Done with laundry."
- "Push the oil change to next week."
- "Take the passport renewal off my list."

Or use the **Oso** list in the Google Tasks app on your phone. Check things off, add new ones, or edit them there. Oso picks up your changes on its next check, within about 15 minutes. Changes made through Claude show up on your phone right away.

To-dos that Oso finds in your email, GroupMe, and Canvas, like "sign the housing form," land on the list on their own.

**Reminders at a specific moment** work differently. "Remind me to swing by the mail room on my way back to the dorm" isn't a task. It's a pop-up. Claude looks at your class schedule and sets a reminder for when your class lets out. It tells you the time: "I'll remind you at 10:45, when Physics lets out."

## Getting the most out of it

- **Dump everything in.** Ten things for this week in no particular order is exactly what it's for. You don't have to plan them.
- **Let the briefing pick.** Each morning it lists what's open, overdue first. It also shows your free time left today and suggests one to three tasks that fit.
- **Ask what fits right now.** "I have an hour, what should I knock out?" gets a suggestion based on your schedule.
- **Check things off as you go,** on your phone or by telling Claude. The briefing mentions what you finished.
- **Keep coursework out of it.** Homework, readings, and exams belong on your deadlines list, where grade weights and due times matter. See [Deadlines and alerts](deadlines-and-alerts.md).

## Setting it up

Tasks works through Claude and the briefing right away. To have the list on your phone, connect Google Tasks. See [Install guide: Google Tasks](../install.md#google-tasks). Then install the free Google Tasks app. Tasks with a day also show up in Google Calendar.

## Good to know

- Oso only touches the list it made, named Oso. Your other Google Tasks lists are left alone.
- Google Tasks keeps a day for each task, not a time. That's why reminders at a moment are calendar pop-ups instead.
- If you change the same task on your phone and through Claude between checks, the newer change wins.
