# Grades

The **Grades** tab in the Oso window shows every class you're taking this semester in one table: your grade in each, lowest first, and a note on the work pulling it down.

## What it's for

Canvas shows grades one course at a time. The Grades tab puts them side by side, so the class that needs attention is at the top and you can see why without clicking through assignments.

## How to use it

Open the Oso window (say "open my settings" in the Claude app, or open **Oso** from your Start menu) and click **Grades**. Each row has:

- **Course**: the class.
- **Grade**: your current grade as Canvas shows it, with the letter when the instructor posts one. When an instructor hides the overall grade, Oso shows the share of points you've earned so far, marked as an estimate.
- **Note**: up to two pieces of work that are hurting the grade, worst first: anything scored under 70%, and anything Canvas marks late or missing.

The line at the bottom says when Oso last read Canvas. **Refresh** reloads the table after a check.

## Getting the most out of it

- It needs Oso signed in to [Canvas](canvas.md). The calendar feed alone carries no grades.
- An estimate counts every point the same, while instructors often weight categories (exams more than homework). Treat it as a rough figure, and ask Claude "how am I doing in history?" for the weighted picture and what you need on the rest. See [Where you stand](where-you-stand.md).
- Orientation modules and trainings (Title IX and the like) are left out, and so are classes from past semesters and courses you've marked finished.
