# School messages

Oso reads your school email, your GroupMe groups, and your instructors' Canvas announcements and inbox messages. It picks out what affects your schedule or your to-do list: a deadline, an event, something you need to do, or a change to one of them.

## What it's for

A lot of what competes for your time never shows up in Canvas assignments. An instructor emails that a deadline moved. The registrar says registration opens Monday. Your club's GroupMe moves tonight's meeting to the soccer fields. A Canvas announcement says to bring your lab kit. Oso catches these so they reach your calendar, your task list, and your morning briefing, instead of getting buried in an inbox you skim.

## How to use it

Once your accounts are connected, it runs on its own every 15 minutes. Claude reads each new message and keeps only the facts that matter:

- **Deadlines** join your deadlines list. A message that moves or cancels one updates it, and an urgent change reaches your calendar. See [Deadlines and alerts](deadlines-and-alerts.md).
- **Events** (meetings, review sessions, practices, socials) go on the Oso calendar, marked with where they came from.
- **Things to do** that aren't coursework (sign a form, pay dues, register) go on your [task list](tasks.md).
- **Class notes** (a class canceled, moved, or online; something to bring; something to read first) change that class on your calendar and lead your briefing. See [Class schedule](class-schedule.md).

Most messages don't matter, like newsletters, chatter, and ads, and are skipped.

You can control what it reads:

- "Stop reading the memes group."
- "Stop reading email from the bookstore."
- "Start reading the Baja group again."

You can also tell Claude about things that reach you some other way: "add Saturday's tailgate, noon at the stadium."

## Getting the most out of it

- **Forward your school email to your Oso Gmail.** Oso reads one Gmail account. Set up a forwarding rule in your school email so everything lands there. Oso unwraps forwarded messages, so it still sees who sent each one.
- **Mute noisy groups early.** Every GroupMe group is read until you mute it, and muting the busy social ones saves your Claude usage.
- **Sign in to Canvas.** With the Canvas sign-in, Oso reads announcements and messages straight from Canvas, which is where "bring your Arduino kit" usually shows up. See [Canvas](canvas.md).
- **Check the briefing's heads-up section.** That's where messages turn into decisions, like an event the night before an exam.

## Good to know

- Oso keeps the facts and a link back to the message, never the message itself. Once a message has been read, its text is dropped and nothing is copied into your vault.
- Oso never sends, deletes, or changes anything in your email, GroupMe, or Canvas. It reads Canvas messages without marking them as read.
- Ads, social-network notices, and Canvas's own notification emails are set aside without being read.
- Reading messages uses some of your Claude plan. If it uses too much, set a daily limit in the Oso window's Settings tab. There's no limit by default.
- Only recent announcements and Canvas messages, from the last few days, are read for changes. Older ones are already history.
