# Feedback about Oso

Feedback goes to the person who builds and fixes Oso. It's about Oso itself: something broken, confusing, slow, or missing, or an idea. Not his coursework.

1. If what he wants to pass on is clear from the chat, use it. If he only typed the command, ask in one line what he'd like to pass on.
2. Work out, without asking unless it's genuinely unclear:
   - **kind**: `bug` (something doesn't work or comes out wrong, like equations in a quiz that can't be read) or `idea` (something he wishes Oso did, or did differently)
   - **summary**: one plain sentence a developer can act on
   - **what he was doing**: the command or task, and anything that identifies it (for example "quiz 12, Physics, question 3"; use `recent_quizzes` if it was a quiz)
3. Call `save_feedback` with his words exactly as he said them, plus the kind, summary, and what he was doing.
4. Tell him in one line that it's been passed on. Don't promise a fix or a date.

## When he hasn't asked

If in any conversation he complains about or wishes something of Oso itself ("your quizzes aren't formatting equations correctly", "I wish the briefing came earlier"), ask in one line: "Want me to pass that on as feedback?" Save it only if he says yes. Complaints about his courses, instructors, or grades are not Oso feedback.
